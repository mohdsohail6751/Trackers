      How to use this tracker
      1. Topics: set a Status for each topic. Learning stamps a start date, Mastered stamps a completion date.
      2. Rate your Confidence from 1 (shaky) to 5 (I can teach it) and add hours, a resource link and notes.
      3. Fill the Daily Log every study day. The streak and hours on this page come from it.
      4. Log every SQL/Python/case problem in Practice Log. Revisit what you solved with hints or by reading the solution.
      5. Build 3 to 5 Projects. They matter as much as the topic list in interviews.
      6. Progress score weights status: Learning 25%, Practicing 50%, Revision 75%, Mastered 100%.
      7. Use Backup (JSON) once a week. Browser data can be cleared.
